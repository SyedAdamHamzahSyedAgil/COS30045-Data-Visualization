Energy Consumption in Australia

Household appliances are an important part of everyday life in Australia. Appliances such as televisions, refrigerators, washing machines and air conditioners can contribute significantly to household electricity consumption.

The amount of energy an appliance uses depends on several factors, including its power rating, how frequently it is used, how long it operates and its overall energy efficiency. Understanding these factors can help households reduce unnecessary electricity consumption.

Energy Efficiency

Energy-efficient appliances can provide the same functionality while using less electricity.

Usage Habits

How often and how long an appliance is used can have a major impact on its total energy consumption.

Smart Choices

Comparing appliance energy ratings can help consumers make more informed purchasing decisions.

Why Energy Consumption Matters

Electricity consumption affects both household expenses and the environment. Reducing unnecessary energy use can help households manage electricity costs while also supporting Australia's broader energy-efficiency goals.

Energy labels and product specifications can provide useful information when comparing appliances. Consumers can use this information to consider both the initial purchase price and the potential ongoing electricity costs.
