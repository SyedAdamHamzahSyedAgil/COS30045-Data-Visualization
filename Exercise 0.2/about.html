Our Website

The Appliance Energy Consumption Website has been created to provide simple and accessible information about household appliance electricity consumption in the Australian market.

The website focuses on helping users understand some of the factors that influence appliance energy consumption and how everyday usage habits can affect electricity use.

Our Purpose

Education

Provide users with basic information about appliance energy consumption.

Awareness

Encourage greater awareness of electricity consumption in Australian households.

Efficiency

Highlight ways consumers can consider energy efficiency when using or purchasing appliances.

What We Cover

The website currently provides general information about appliance energy consumption and a specific focus on televisions.

Future versions of the website could include additional appliance categories such as refrigerators, washing machines, air conditioners and clothes dryers.
