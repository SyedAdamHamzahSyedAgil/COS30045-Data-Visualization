Televisions in Australian Homes

Televisions are common household appliances in Australia and can consume electricity whenever they are operating. The amount of electricity consumed varies depending on the television model, screen technology, screen size and how long the television is used.

Modern televisions can be designed to operate more efficiently than older models. However, the electricity used over time still depends heavily on individual viewing habits.

Factors Affecting Television Energy Use

Screen Size

Larger screens may require more electricity to operate, although efficiency varies between individual models.

Display Technology

Different display technologies can have different electricity requirements during normal operation.

Viewing Time

A television that is used for many hours each day will generally consume more electricity than one used less frequently.

Standby Mode

Some televisions continue to use a small amount of electricity while in standby mode.

Tips for Reducing Television Energy Use

  • Turn the television off when it is not being watched.
  • Reduce unnecessary viewing time.
  • Consider energy efficiency when purchasing a new television.
  • Use available power-saving settings.
  • Consider switching off the television at the power point when appropriate.
